Preliminary schedule of workshops

Workshops will be held 14th and 16th of April. On each day two workshops will be running in parallel for full day with coffee and lunch breaks. Here are the preliminary timings that all workshops will follow:
  • 09:30 Morning session start
  • 11:00 Morning coffee break
  • 11:30 Morning session continues
  • 13:00 Lunch
  • 14:30 Afternoon session start
  • 16:30 End of the day
Here are the workshops that are planned to run on each day:

Monday, 14 April

  • AI for science (Organisers: Denis Therien and his team)
  • Causality and complex systems (Organisers: Sara Magliacane, Virginia Aglietti)

Wednesday, 16 April

  • Communication of uncertainty (Organisers: Sylvie Delacroix, Umang Bhatt, Diana Robinson)
  • LLMs for social impact (Organisers: Morine Amutorine, Jennifer Schooling)
